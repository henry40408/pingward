Why you're seeing this

A session cookie proves who opened this browser, not who is at it now. These three actions hand out access that keeps working after you sign out, so they need a second confirmation before they run:

Creating a user Resetting another user's password Granting admin rights

Actions that remove access — disabling, demoting, deleting — never ask, so you can always shut an account down in a hurry.
